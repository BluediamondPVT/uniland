Perched dramatically above the Pacific Ocean, the Clifftop Horizon Villa is a masterpiece of contemporary California architecture. Designed by award-winning studio Meridian Architects, this estate commands breathtaking 270° ocean views from nearly every room.

The open-concept living spaces flow effortlessly through floor-to-ceiling glass walls onto expansive terraced decks, blurring the line between interior luxury and the natural landscape. The chef's kitchen features Bulthaup cabinetry, a La Cornue range, and a seamless Calacatta marble island.

The primary suite occupies the entire east wing, with a private terrace, dual dressing rooms, and a spa bath anchored by a freestanding soaking tub positioned to capture the horizon at sunrise. Four additional bedroom suites, each with en-suite baths and private balconies, provide generous accommodation for family and guests.
