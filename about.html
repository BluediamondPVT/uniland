Our Story

Two decades of putting clients first.

Victoria Lane founded UniLand in 2003 from a single Manhattan office with three agents and a simple belief: that real estate transactions should be honest, fast, and deeply personal. Twenty-two years later, that belief drives everything we do.

We've grown to 340 agents across New York, London, Dubai, and Singapore — but our culture hasn't changed. Every client gets a dedicated specialist, direct communication, and a team that treats your property like their own.

  • Ranked #1 boutique agency in New York for 5 consecutive years
  • Over $15.4B in successfully closed transactions
  • 98% client satisfaction score — independently verified
